OCR DATE - HIKROBOT HARDWARE TRIGGER
====================================

LUONG HOAT DONG
PLC/cam bien -> day trigger -> Line0/Line1 cua camera Hikrobot
Camera -> GigE/USB -> MVS SDK -> OCR DATE -> PASS/FAIL tren man hinh

Che do san xuat chi ARM camera va cho frame do tin hieu dien ben ngoai tao ra.
Nut TRIGGER TEST (SOFTWARE) chi phat mot frame chan doan roi tra ve Line0/Line1.

CAI TREN MAY GAN CAMERA
1. Cai HIKROBOT MVS 64-bit va Python SDK cua MVS.
2. Mo MVS, chac chan thay camera, focus ro va chup duoc o Hardware Trigger.
3. Dat Pixel Format la Mono8 (khuyen dung), Bayer8, RGB8 Packed hoac BGR8 Packed.
4. Neu Python SDK khong o duong dan mac dinh, tao bien moi truong:
   HIKROBOT_MVIMPORT=C:\...\MvImport
   Thu muc nay phai chua MvCameraControl_class.py.
5. Mo OCR_DATE.exe, chon "Camera Hikrobot - trigger ngoai", nhap serial (co the
   de trong neu chi co mot camera), chon Line0/Line1 va bam KET NOI + ARM.
6. Dung CAMERA LIVE de chinh lens/exposure, sau do DUNG LIVE de khoi phuc trigger.
7. Chi khi man hinh bao "DA ARM - CHO TRIGGER" moi cho may chay.

AN TOAN DU LIEU
- Khong tu doan ky tu. ROI/OCR/confidence/ngay/AB/DM/goc/thoi gian sai deu FAIL.
- So frame SDK bi nhay coc -> FAIL_FRAME_GAP.
- Moc 100 ms tinh tu luc SDK giao buffer anh toi luc co quyet dinh; khong bao gom
  exposure, truyen anh truoc SDK, PLC va co cau chap hanh.

DA KIEM TRA
- 39 unit test.
- GUI anh tinh tren 5 anh mau.
- GUI 13 anh cam tay va cong loi lech goc.
- Camera Hikrobot DA9982258 da chup va replay: 45/45 PASS, doc dung 45/45,
  tim dai toi 45/45, lon nhat 55,54 ms tren may phat trien.
- ONNX CPU: 130/130 ket qua dung; benchmark 250/250 duoi 100 ms.

CHUA KIEM TRA
- Dien ap/canh trigger, chu ky lien tuc va toc do day chuyen thuc te.
- Hieu nang tren may dich Core i5 Gen 8 / RAM 8 GB.

Ban CPU Lite dung ONNX Runtime CPU 4 luong, khong can GPU roi, CUDA hay PyTorch.
Can nghiem thu lai voi dung model camera, lens, den, do phan giai, exposure, CPU,
cap va chu ky trigger truoc khi cho phep day chuyen tu dong.
